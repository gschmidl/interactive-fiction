[31] Riverview

pestiferous spirits).  As far as the eye can see, in either direction,
the river is uncrossable.  The far north bank is edged by a heavily
overgrown field dense in timothy and flora unlike any other.  The field

various wild animals graze insouciantly on the riparian plants.  To
the southwest is a dirt path.  Through a clearing in the trees on the
southern bank, in the distance, can be seen an unaturally straight
edge of reddish-brown.
