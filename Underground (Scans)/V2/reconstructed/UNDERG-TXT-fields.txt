UNDERG.TXT — message fields, reconstructed from the scans
=========================================================

Format: <index><TAB><text of that 73-byte field>

Each index addresses ONE 73-byte field, as FNR$(A%) in UNDERG.BAS does:
  record A%\7 + 1  (512-byte blocks), field A% MOD 7, 73 bytes, CVT$$ trimmed.
Long messages therefore span CONSECUTIVE indices and the game prints them with
  &FNR$(Z%); FOR Z%= X% TO Y%
so field text often breaks mid-word. That is faithful, not an error.

"@" is the item-name placeholder (author's own term). In the byte-offset
printouts (07192619/20) it was accidentally printed as the literal string
"Password:" by a stale variable; it is restored to "@" here.

Sources (all V2): 07192616 (fields 0-43), 07192615 (15-139),
07192617 (170-223), 07192614 (267-443). Where two printouts overlap they
agree exactly — 07192616 and 07192615 both give 15-43 identically.
Gaps where no indexed printout survives are marked  ### GAP ###.

NOT used as a source: 07192619/07192620 and 07192613. Those are a LATER
revision (the unfinished V3): they reword messages ("full of chemical
pollutants and unsafe to drink" vs V2's "polluted and unfit to drink"),
correct spellings (Entrance vs V2's Enterance), add rooms V2 has no trace of
(Spectre's Room, a demon, an "Underground Command Interpreter"), and replace
V2's one-message-per-object text with "@" templates ("There is a @ here.").
Their field numbering is therefore NOT V2's, and filling V2 indices from them
would be invention rather than recovery.
