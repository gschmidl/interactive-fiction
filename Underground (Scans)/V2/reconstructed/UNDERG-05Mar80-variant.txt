1. TAIL OF THE MARCH LISTING (lines 16200-17000)
=====================================================================

Lines struck through in pen are marked [CROSSED OUT]. They implement a
"CAVEMN.DAT" second-cave / restore feature that does NOT appear in the
20-May-80 listing.

            X%=X%+15% IF O%(Y%)=2% FOR Y%=1% TO 20%: RETURN
16300 Z$=SYS("^E")
16500 &fnr$(X%); for X%=431% to 446%: B%=1%: goto 15900
16505 GOTO 18000                                        [CROSSED OUT]
16600 INPUT #1%"HELLO";A$                               [CROSSED OUT]
16700 IF A$="HELLO"THEN B1$="CAVEMN.DAT":GOTO5100       [CROSSED OUT]
16800 B1$="CAVEMN.DAT":GOTO5000                         [CROSSED OUT]
17000 GOTO16600                                         [CROSSED OUT]
16600                       END      ["66" written in pen over the
                                      original line number]

Note: the 20-May-80 listing keeps 16300/16500 and ends at "16600 END";
16400 V$=SYS("^NBYE/F") is present in May but not visible on this sheet.
