have done so far, say "SCORE".  To get full credit for the treasures
you have found, you must bring them to safety inside the house, though
you get part credit just for finding them. Points are deducted for
getting killed and for quitting early, most of all for the former. Your
score also depends on how much of the cave (if any) you have managed to
explore; in particular there is a big bonus for getting into the cave
(to tell the beginners from the rest of the pack), and there are other
ways to find out if you have been through some of the harder places.

longer. If nothing happens, you have not yet found them all. If
something interesting *does* happen, it means you get a bonus and have
the chance to score many more points in the masters' section. I may
give you a hint now and then if you seem to have got into difficulties.
If I do, I will warn you in advance how many points it will cost you.

means you get a full description of the places you are in only when you
ask for it specially.
