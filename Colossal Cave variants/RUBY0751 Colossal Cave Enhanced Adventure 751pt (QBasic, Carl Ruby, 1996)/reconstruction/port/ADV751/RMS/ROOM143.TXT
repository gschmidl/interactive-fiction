castle built against the southern face of an unscalable granite 
cliff.  On your left an impregnable wall of gleaming white stone 
encompasses the entire court and castle in a half-moon shaped curve 
"which meets the cliff at either end.  To your right, across the" 
"steeply rising courtyard, is Lords' Keep, the heart of the castle."
