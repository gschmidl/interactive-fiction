. Univac 1100 series MASM (Meta Assembler) routine to give current time
. and date to FORTRAN program in TDATE$ format.
.
.        CALL TDATE(I)
.
. See FORTRAN subroutine DATIME for details of bit meanings.
.
$(1)      .
tdate*    .
          s         a0,a2             . save A0
          er        tdate$            . obtain current time and date in A0
          tz,s1     0,x11             . called from ASCII FORTRAN ?
          j         tdftn             . yes
          L         A1,0,X11          . no (FORTRAN-V) - get addr of argument
          S         A0,0,A1           . store time and date in user's argument
          J         2,X11             . return to FORTRAN-V program
tdftn     .
          l         a1,0,a2           . get addr of argument
          s         a0,0,a1           . store time and date in user's argument
          j         0,x11             . return to FTN program
.
          END
