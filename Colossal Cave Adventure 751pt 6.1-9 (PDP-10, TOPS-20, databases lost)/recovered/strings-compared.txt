Wording compiled into the two builds, compared.

Only the program's own text is compared -- FORMAT literals and character
constants.  A stretch is listed when none of its words sits in a run of
3 consecutive words that the other build also contains, so the same
sentence split into literals differently still counts as shared.

Constants shorter than five characters are not extracted: next to
machine code, a three-letter word cannot be told from noise.  So a word
table can be listed here for one build only because a short entry beside
it (THE, SAFE, IT) was not picked up in the other.  The event names of
ENVIRN are the same thirty, in the same order, in both builds.
