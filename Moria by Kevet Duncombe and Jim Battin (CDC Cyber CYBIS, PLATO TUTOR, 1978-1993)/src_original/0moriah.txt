                   to steal.  Cunning characters get the
                   edge of surprise over most monsters
                   and can quite often trick monsters.
                   Cunning also affects the chance of
                   opening boxes and chests successfully
                   and the chance of evading monsters.
 
           Piety:  Wisdom or ability to pray.  Determines
                   the success rate and level of praying
                   you are able to do.  Also affects the
                   casting of some peacetime spells.

                   fighting and decreases damage done to a
                   character by the monsters.  Valor also
                   determines which weapons a character
                   can use since some weapons require more
                   skill to use than others.
 
        Wizardry:  Intelligence or skill in casting spells.
                   Wizardry determines the level of magic
                   you are able to safely cast, along with
                   the chances of success.
