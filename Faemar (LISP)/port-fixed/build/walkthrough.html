00 Before you start

Four things the code does that no amount of exploring in-game will reveal:

◆ The game never ends

There's no victory message, no final score readout, no command anywhere in the source that checks what you're holding or where you've been and declares you done. START's main loop just keeps reading commands forever. If you reach the Shrine and take the idol's eyes expecting something to happen — nothing does. quit is the only ending there is.

◆ There is no key, anywhere

Four doors are flagged %LOCKED% (DR5, D11, D12, D13), and each has an unlock handler — but the object KEY it checks for is never defined anywhere in the world data. Every unlock attempt fails, permanently. The lock is decorative — break these doors like any other. Breaking doesn't check the lock flag at all.

◆ One corridor goes nowhere

The Dark Intersection's south exit is wired to a room called HL8 that was never built — no description, no contents, nothing. Walking south from there just returns to an empty stub. This is a gap in the surviving 1982 source, not something you're missing.

◆ The fountain's coins are a trap for the eye

The coins glittering in the Fountain Room belong to an object literally named FAKE-COINS, with infinite weight baked in. The game even tells you so as you try: “some of the coins disappear”. Don't waste turns on them — the real treasure is elsewhere.

03 Phase 1 · Up the hill

1.1 From the hillside to the entrance

Get inside and arm yourself. The outer door is already open — nothing to break yet.

  1. You start at the Outside Complex, facing the stairway. Go n.
  2. At the Base of the Stairs: take mace. Then n.
  3. At the Top of the Stairs, the doorway (DR1) starts unlocked and open — just go n into the Entrance.

04 Phase 2 · The entrance hub

2.1 Three doors, one room

The Entrance is the hub everything else branches from — north, east and west all lead to fresh territory, each behind its own door.

  1. Optional flavor: examine flask, examine twigs — wind-blown debris, nothing more.
  2. Breaking through: break door with mace works on whichever door you're facing — the parser only asks “Which door?” when more than one is in reach, so add a direction when it does, e.g. break north door with mace. Doors take several hits (their strength is rolled randomly, roughly 11–20); keep swinging until it collapses.

    Naming the weapon skips wielding entirely — see the wielding note under Quirks before you try move mace on hand.

05 Phase 3 · The eastern loop

3.1 Into the well-lit intersection

  1. From the Entrance: break east door with mace, then e, e to reach the Lighted Intersection.

3.2 The rat tunnels — the real treasure

This loop connects back to the Meeting Room on its own, so it's worth doing before you ever touch the Meeting Room's door.

  1. s to the Trashy Hallway, then s again into the Trash Room.
  2. examine trash if you like — it describes the hole you're about to use (the parser doesn't actually know the word “junk”, only trash, garbage, or pile). Then ne to squeeze into Rat's Crawl.
  3. e into the Rat's Lair. take treasure — this is the +50 score object.
  4. n, n to squeeze up through the second crawl straight into the Meeting Room — no door needed.
  5. Look around: examine fireplace, examine ashes, examine chairs, examine benches. All flavor, no items.

3.3 Back to the hub, then the north hallway

  1. If you want the Sooty Hallway/Meeting-Room door open too (not required — the crawl already connects them), it's break door with mace from either side.
  2. Return the way you came, or via the crawl, to the Lighted Intersection, then n to the Access Hallway.
  3. break door with mace, then n into an Access Room (RM4) — cobwebs in the corner, nothing to take.

06 Phase 4 · The northern vault

4.1 Through the Northern Hall

  1. Back at the Entrance: break north door with mace, then n to the Northern Hall.
  2. Two doors are in reach here — the one you came through, and the one ahead. The next one is locked and breakable, but the lock doesn't matter: break north door with mace, then n into the Access Room (ACR).

4.2 The secret door

A hidden passage back down to the Base of the Stairs. You don't have to wait for the random hint to notice it — the word secret matches it directly.

  1. examine hilt first — the broken dagger is a clue that someone already tried this.
  2. break secret door with mace — this works immediately, whether or not the room has “detected” it yet for you. Keep swinging (its strength is rolled tougher than the others, roughly 11–30).
  3. This is a one-way shortcut: ne drops you back at the Base of the Stairs, but there's no return route coded from that side. Use it as a fast trip out, not a loop.

4.3 The other access rooms

  1. From the Access Room: break east door with mace, e reaches the other Access Room (RM4) if you skipped it earlier.
  2. break west door with mace, w reaches a third Access Room (RM6) with a burnt-out torch stub — this is the same room you can also reach from the western wing below.

07 Phase 5 · The western wing

5.1 Into the dark intersection

  1. From the Entrance: break west door with mace, w, w to the Dark Intersection.
  2. s leads to the unbuilt HL8 stub — a dead end left over from the archived source. Nothing there; turn back.

5.2 To the fountain and beyond

  1. w to the Wet Hallway, then break door with mace, w into the Fountain Room.
  2. examine fountain for flavor. Don't bother trying to take the coins — see Before you start.

08 Phase 6 · The shrine (read first)

⚠ A dead end, not an ending

The door into the Shrine locks itself the instant you step through, and the code gives it no way to be reopened afterward (it's no longer breakable, and “open” is refused outright). Do everything else in this walkthrough first.

And temper your expectations once you're in: taking the eyes doesn't trigger anything. EYES and IDOL carry no VERBS property at all in the source — picking them up runs the same generic “you take it” logic as any other object. There is, in fact, no win condition or congratulatory message anywhere in the archived code, and no command ever prints the score it's been quietly tracking. quit is the only way the game ends.

  1. From the Fountain Room: break west door with mace — you'll hear a soft gong as it gives way.
  2. w into the Shrine. The door shuts behind you as you enter.
  3. examine idol, then take eyes — the idol's two red gems, a trophy with nothing further attached to it.

09 Quirks & dead ends

Behavior worth knowing about before you meet it blind — all genuine artifacts of the 1982 code, confirmed by reading the relevant handlers side by side.

no ending

There is no win condition anywhere in the source — no victory message, no final score, nothing that checks what you're carrying or where you've been. START's command loop just keeps reading input forever. quit is the only way out, whether you've taken every item or none of them.

wielding bug

move mace on hand triggers an infinite loop in the Hand object's own verb handler: it calls the generic move routine before clearing the state that re-triggers that same handler. move mace to hand (or in hand) hits a different, correctly-written branch and works fine — or skip wielding entirely and just say break door with mace, which never touches this code path.

no key

Four doors check for an object called KEY that was never defined in the world data. unlock always fails on them — break them instead, which ignores the lock flag entirely.

one-way shrine

Covered above: the Shrine's door cannot be reopened once broken through and entered. It's a dead end, not a scripted finale — see “no ending” above.

unbuilt room

The Dark Intersection's south exit points at a room (HL8) that exists only as a name in a word list — no room was ever written for it.

no scoreboard

Taking the Rat's Treasure adds 50 to a SCORE property on the player — but no command in the source ever prints it back out. The points exist only in memory, never on screen.
