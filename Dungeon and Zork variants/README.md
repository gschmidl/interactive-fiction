# Dungeon and Zork variants

- [Dungeon (Burroughs B7700, MCP, FORTRAN source)](Dungeon%20%28Burroughs%20B7700%2C%20MCP%2C%20FORTRAN%20source%29/) - The FORTRAN *Dungeon* V2.0 converted for a Burroughs B7700 (1980): the main game, no endgame.
- [Dungeon (HP 1000, RTE, FORTRAN source)](Dungeon%20%28HP%201000%2C%20RTE%2C%20FORTRAN%20source%29/) - Dungeon V3.0a for the HP 1000 (1982), by way of the Burroughs version; the main game only.
- [Dungeon (HP 3000, MPE)](Dungeon%20%28HP%203000%2C%20MPE%29/) - The FORTRAN *Dungeon* as installed on an HP 3000.
- [Qork V3.0A (CDC NOS, 1984, FORTRAN source)](Qork%20V3.0A%20%28CDC%20NOS%2C%201984%2C%20FORTRAN%20source%29/) - S. O. Lidie's *Qork*, the DECUS *Dungeon* for a CDC Cyber, with his own 600-point ending.
- [Zork (Data General MV, AOS-VS)](Zork%20%28Data%20General%20MV%2C%20AOS-VS%29/) - An AOS/VS *Zork* of 1984, the *Dungeon* version with its endgame.
