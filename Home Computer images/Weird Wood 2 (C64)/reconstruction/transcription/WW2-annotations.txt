WEIRD WOOD II - HANDWRITTEN ANNOTATIONS
=======================================
Everything written by hand on the two PDFs, page by page.  The annotator was
working through the listing in order to re-implement / port it: they label the
verb handlers with the verb name, note which .DAT file each GOSUB 650 pulls
in, flag bugs, and sketch replacement code.

Readings are best-effort; genuinely illegible words are marked <?>.
