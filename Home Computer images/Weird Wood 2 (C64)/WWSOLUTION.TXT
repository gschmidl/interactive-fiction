* Survival (not in the original walkthrough but you'll die without it)
- thirst: outdoors, in caves, and especially in the desert you get thirsty over time. Fill the bottle at any water source and DRINK WATER when you start feeling thirsty - leave it too long and you die
- hunger: eat food now and then; ignore the pangs and you starve
- food poisoning: the prison sandwich is rotten - eating it starts a clock that will kill you. Only eat it when you can head straight to the hospital to be healed
- exhaustion: if your health is low, lie on the bed or couch in your cell to recover
- battery: the torch eats its battery while lit. Turn it off in lit rooms or it goes flat and leaves you stranded in the dark
- rollcall: until you're a trustee you have to be back in your cell when rollcall is announced or you're sent to solitary confinement
- orc pursuit: once you're a trustee an orc starts hunting you. If you hear pursuit close behind, kill it with the sword or it'll drag you back to solitary

* Top score

The score is out of 1000. Treasures you're carrying when the game ends count;
treasures dropped in the cargo hold of the spaceship count DOUBLE. The trustee
gifts you give the Governor and the parts consumed building the matter transmitter
also count double, so all the things you collect along the main route are already
scoring well - you just have to remember to drop everything you're still carrying
into the cargo hold before you fly.

There are twenty treasures in all - if every one of them ends up with the Governor,
in the matter transmitter, or in the cargo hold, that's 400 points. The five
trustee gifts (pyramid, carving, cornucopia, harp, phial) and the five matter
transmitter parts (widget, circuit board, photon driver, tachyon converter, luxon
generator) are all collected and scored as part of the main walkthrough. The sword
also gets collected to deal with the orc - just remember to drop it in the cargo
hold afterwards.

These extra treasures aren't needed to finish the game but are worth picking up
to make up the rest of the 400:

  - bar of gold: at the bottom of a deep well shaft
  - diamond: a small cave deep in the cavern system
  - ruby: a north-south passage with rough rock walls in the caves
  - emerald: a small cave with three tunnels leading deeper
  - sapphire: outdoors by a narrow river that flows south over weirs
  - silver ingot: in the hayloft
  - crystal chalice: in the small chapel - grab it while you're collecting the photon driver
  - phylactery (the amulet): you've already worn this to get into the chapel; pick it back up afterwards instead of leaving it behind
  - the SHOOTING STARS game itself: take it from the bar after you've won the pass

For a perfect 1000 you also need a perfect score on the shooting stars game and
to roam every region - each new outdoor / forest / cavern area you step into bumps
the trustee bonus a little, and that bonus is what closes the gap from "finished"
to "1000".
