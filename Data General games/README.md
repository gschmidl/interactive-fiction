# Data General games

- [Dungeons (Data General, AOS)](Dungeons%20%28Data%20General%2C%20AOS%29/) - "Rev. 4 of the Dungeon game" (1980): carry the Power Ring out of a maze that is new every game.
- [Ferret (Data General MV, AOS-VS)](Ferret%20%28Data%20General%20MV%2C%20AOS-VS%29/) - Ferret Rev 4.10, a PL/I adventure by Data General's UK Systems Division (from 1982).
- [Thissala (Data General Eclipse, AOS-VS)](Thissala%20%28Data%20General%20Eclipse%2C%20AOS-VS%29/) - *Thissala* (1985), by three of Data General's Corporate Consulting Group.
