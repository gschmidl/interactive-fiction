New Adventure -- walkable map
Mark D. Niemiec, University of Waterloo, 1979 (Copyright (C) 1979).

The engine and the object code are lost; this walks the 162 recovered
locations only.  Nothing here is invented -- "src" shows you the
original F source behind anything the walker will not simulate.
Type "help" for commands.

== ROAD_BTM ==
You are standing at the end of a road before a small brick building, Around
you is a forest. A small stream flows out of the building and down a gully.
There are some bushes to the north.

ROAD_BTM> exits
  W U ROAD HILL -> ROAD_TOP
  E BUILD IN -> WELL_HOUSE
  N BUSHES -> ORCH_BUSH
  S VALLEY DOWNSTREAM (DEPRESSION) (GRATE) -> STREAM_VALLEY
  NW SE NE SW FOREST -> -FOREST_1

ROAD_BTM> e

== WELL_HOUSE ==
You are inside a building, a well house for a large spring.

WELL_HOUSE> w

== ROAD_BTM ==
You are standing at the end of a road before a small brick building, Around
you is a forest. A small stream flows out of the building and down a gully.
There are some bushes to the north.

ROAD_BTM> s

== STREAM_VALLEY ==
You are in a valley in the forest beside a stream tumbling along a rocky
bed.

STREAM_VALLEY> exits
  N BUILD UPSTREAM -> ROAD_BTM
  E W U FOREST -> -FOREST_1
  DOWNSTREAM D W S SLIT (DEPRESSION) (GRATE) -> SLIT_2IN

STREAM_VALLEY> d

== SLIT_2IN ==
At your feet all the water of the stream splashes into a 2-inch slit in the
rock. Downstream the streambed is bare rock.

SLIT_2IN> go ABOVE_GRATE

== ABOVE_GRATE ==
You are in a 20-foot depression floored with bare dirt. Set into the dirt is
a strong steel grate mounted in concrete. A dry streambed leads into the
depression

ABOVE_GRATE> exits
  D IN -> guarded (BELOW_GRATE)
  E W S FOREST -> -FOREST_1
  N VALLEY (BUILD) (ROAD) (HILL) -> SLIT_2IN
  D IN GRATE -> BELOW_GRATE

ABOVE_GRATE> d
  [guarded: this exit depends on game state the walker does not
   model.  loc.f says:]
    | switch(.GRATE) {
    | default:
    | 	"You can't go through a locked steel grate!\n";
    | 1:
    | 	"You can't go through a closed steel grate!\n";
    | 2:
    | 	BELOW_GRATE;
    | }
  ["force" to go anyway]

ABOVE_GRATE> force
  [forced -- the original would have tested the condition above]

== BELOW_GRATE ==
You are in a small chamber beneath a 3x3 steel grate to the surface. A small
passage leads downwards to the east.

BELOW_GRATE> exits
  U OUT -> guarded (ABOVE_GRATE)
  U DEPRESSION OUT -> ABOVE_GRATE
  E D -> GRATE_E

BELOW_GRATE> path CAVE_TOP
  8 steps: BELOW_GRATE -> GRATE_E -> BALL_RM -> CAVE_WW -> CAVE_W -> CAVE_BTM -> CAVE_LO -> CAVE_HI -> CAVE_TOP

BELOW_GRATE> go TREE_FRONT

== STONE_DOOR ==   (no LIGHT in Furnish)
  [Look depends on game state; loc.f says:]
    | if(.STONE_DOOR)
    | 	"The way east is through an open door\n";
    | else
    | 	"The way east is barred by what appears to be a solid stone wall.
    | 	There is a message written high up on the wall.\n";

STONE_DOOR> read
The message reads, 'Speak, friend, and enter.'

STONE_DOOR> e
  [guarded: this exit depends on game state the walker does not
   model.  loc.f says:]
    | if(.STONE_DOOR)
    | 	STATUE_RM;
    | else
    | 	"There is no way to go in that direction.\n";
  ["force" to go anyway]

STONE_DOOR> acts
  Look       (code)
  FRIEND     (code)

STONE_DOOR> src FRIEND
    | if(.STONE_DOOR ^= 1)
    | 	"A door opens in the wall.\n";
    | else
    | 	"The door vanishes, leaving a wall of solid rock.\n";
