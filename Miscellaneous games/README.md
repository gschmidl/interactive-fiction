# Miscellaneous games

- [AmigaMUD](AmigaMUD/) - The demonstration world of Chris Gray's AmigaMUD (1991), played single-user.
- [Dave's Dungeon - DAVESCAVE (PDP-11 TRAX, MITRE, FORTRAN source)](Dave's%20Dungeon%20-%20DAVESCAVE%20%28PDP-11%20TRAX%2C%20MITRE%2C%20FORTRAN%20source%29/) - Dave Parker's "Dungeons and Dragons" from MITRE (1980), a dungeon crawl with two-letter commands.
- [Explore 5.3 (Multics, BASIC source)](Explore%205.3%20%28Multics%2C%20BASIC%20source%29/) - Jim Lippard's 500-point cave adventure (1980), reconstructed from his printouts.
- [Faemar (LISP)](Faemar%20%28LISP%29/) - *Faemar* (1982), a text adventure in a MacLisp-family Lisp.
- [Google Adventure (Browser)](Google%20Adventure%20%28Browser%29/) - The text adventure hidden in Google's search page in 2018: collect the letters of the logo.
- [Misty Marsh (Wang OIS)](Misty%20Marsh%20%28Wang%20OIS%29/) - A cave adventure written as a Wang word-processor glossary macro.
- [Mystery Mansion (HP 1000, RTE, FORTRAN source)](Mystery%20Mansion%20%28HP%201000%2C%20RTE%2C%20FORTRAN%20source%29/) - Bill Wolpert's *Mystery Mansion* rev. 16 (1981): a murder in an old mansion, and the day runs out.
- [Six and Fant](Six%20and%20Fant/) - Four worlds in SIX, the University of Alberta's adventure-writing language of the late 1970s.
- [The Sewers of Piing Poong (Teco)](The%20Sewers%20of%20Piing%20Poong%20%28Teco%29/) - A tiny early-1980s adventure written in TECO: sewers, catacombs, a pipe maze and one way out.
- [Unnamed volcano adventure (NorthStar Horizon BASIC, DBACK)](Unnamed%20volcano%20adventure%20%28NorthStar%20Horizon%20BASIC%2C%20DBACK%29/) - An untitled, deliberately unfinished adventure: escape the headhunters down a volcano.
- [Wander worlds + Dune + Beasts (Peter Langston, Usenix 80.1 and 85.1 tapes)](Wander%20worlds%20%2B%20Dune%20%2B%20Beasts%20%28Peter%20Langston%2C%20Usenix%2080.1%20and%2085.1%20tapes%29/) - Peter Langston's Wander worlds, *Dune* and *Beasts* from the Usenix tapes (nothing here runs them).
- [ZK Spitbrook (VAX VMS, full Pascal source)](ZK%20Spitbrook%20%28VAX%20VMS%2C%20full%20Pascal%20source%29/) - *ZK* (1985), interactive fiction set at Digital's Spitbrook Road engineering facility.
- [Zeno (IBM VM-CMS, REXX)](Zeno%20%28IBM%20VM-CMS%2C%20REXX%29/) - Dave Mitchell's *ZENO* (1983): save a deep-space station by programming its computer against the clock.
