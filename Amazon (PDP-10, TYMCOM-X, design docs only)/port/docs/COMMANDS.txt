AMAZON -- commands
==================

The parser is AMAZOT.SAI's: input is upper-cased and split into runs of
[A-Z 0-9 * -].  Everything else is a separator.  One verb per line, then as
many nouns as you like -- GET KNIFE LAMP MATCHES works, because AMAZON.SAI's
ACQUIRE loops over every word that follows the verb.


FROM CMDLIB.SAI (11-Sep-77) -- the original vocabulary
------------------------------------------------------

  class        commands                             strength
  -----        --------                             --------
  ACQUIRE      ACQUIRE GET TAKE CAPTURE KEEP           1
               STEAL ROB                               2
  RELINQUISH   RELINQUISH DROP RELEASE                 1
               THROW TOSS                              2
  DIRECTION    N NE E SE S SW W NW U D                 0-9
               NORTH NORTHEAST EAST SOUTHEAST SOUTH
               SOUTHWEST WEST NORTHWEST UP DOWN

Strength matters.  GET takes what is lying about; STEAL and ROB will take it
out of another player's hands, if your DEXTERITY beats their AGILITY -- and
if a Policeman sees you do it, you will be arrested.  DROP puts a thing down;
THROW and TOSS throw it, which is how you get the jug of water onto the
Wicked Witch and the silver bullet into the Vampire.


ADDED FOR THIS PORT -- not in any surviving file
------------------------------------------------

Looking about
  LOOK / L                  describe where you are
  EXAMINE <thing> / X       print that thing's OBJECT.TYP entry
  READ <thing>              signs, the Law Book, the wizard's diary, and the
                            writing on the walls of the Riddle Room
  INVENTORY / I             what you are carrying, and your load

Doing things
  OPEN / CLOSE <thing>      the desk, the drawers, the boxes
  UNLOCK <thing>            needs the right key set
  SAY <word>                some words do things
  ANSWER <word>             answer a riddle or a Bar Exam question
  PUT <thing>               put it down
  EAT / DRINK <thing>       the cake and the urn are both marked
  ATTACK / KILL <creature>
  KISS <creature>           "If kissed by prince/princess becomes princess"
  TIE <creature>            with the spider web
  RIDE <creature>
  SLEEP                     in sunlight, if the Shadow has you
  LIGHT / EXTINGUISH <lamp>
  LAUNCH                    fire the catapult at the Top of Cliff
  WEAR <thing>

Trade and law
  BUY <thing>               in the Equipment Shop; costs points
  ORDER                     order a drink, and take the Bar Exam
  DELIVER / RETURN <thing>  hand a thing back for its reward
  STUDY                     read the Law Book properly, before the exam
  PLEAD                     in the Jail, to be taken to court;
                            in the Court Room, to be tried
  HINT                      the Frog, the witch and the Lone Ranger all
                            "give hints"

Yourself and the others
  SCORE                     points, treasures returned, deaths
  STATS                     your AMAZON.OFF attribute block
  WHO                       everyone in the valley (PRTEST.SAI's format)
  TELL <words>              say something to everyone
  WAIT / Z
  HELP / H
  SOURCE                    what in this game is original and what is not
  QUIT / DONE / FINISH


GETTING STARTED
---------------

You begin on the Amazon River Bank with nothing and no points, and the shop
wants points.  So earn some first: OBJECT.TYP is full of things that belong
to somebody.  The GREEN Apple in the Elm Grove belongs to Johnny Appleseed,
who is standing right there.  The baby in the basket belongs to Jane.  The
white mice belong in Dr. Livingston's laboratory.

Then buy a light before you go underground, and find out what the Phantom
wants before you try to get past his Wolf.
