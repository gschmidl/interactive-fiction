AMAZON -- the 36 riddles of the Riddle Room (room 1)
====================================================

  ####  RECONSTRUCTED CONTENT -- NOT FROM THE TAPES  ####

Two lines in ROOMS (22-Sep-77) are all that survives of these tables:

    room  1:  "You must answer a riddle to leave (the way you came in)
               ....there are 36 different riddles."
    room 10:  "....*The bartender asks you a question.
               (There are 36 different questions)"

and AMAZON.TXT (09-Sep-79) gives the machinery that indexed them:

    (READ   (DISPLAY  RIDTXT(RIDDLE)))
    (ANSWER (EQUAL (RIDCOD(RIDDLE) INTEXT) ...))

RIDTXT and RIDCOD themselves were never written to any file that survives.
The 36 items below were written for the Windows port so that the room is
playable.  They are drawn only from AMAZON's own material -- every answer is
a room, object or creature named in ROOMS or OBJECT.TYP -- and nothing is
borrowed from any other game.

This file is the plain-text copy of the table in port/src/riddles.c.  If the
originals are ever recovered, replace both and nothing else has to change.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 1.  The witch rides upon me, the black cat sleeps upon me,
     and strange writing runs the length of my handle.  What am I?

     ANSWER: BROOM

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 2.  I belong to a boy who can fly.  I was folded up in a box
     in a girl's room, and I lie flat wherever the light falls.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: SHADOW

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 3.  Kiss me and I am royalty.  Ignore me and I know every answer
     in this room and will not give you one.  What am I?

     ANSWER: FROG

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 4.  I am strong.  When I am good I am silent and I stand guard;
     when I am bad I am loud, and I serve the drinks.  What am I?

     ANSWER: TROLL

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 5.  The bees make me, the field of flowers steals me, and the
     tiny arms will pull you apart to have me.  What am I?

     ANSWER: HONEY

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 6.  I am studded with jewels and I have magical powers, yet all
     I ever do is show you yourself.  A queen of fairyland wants me
     back.  What am I?

     ANSWER: MIRROR

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 7.  I am hungry, I am afraid of light, and I eat whatever is left
     in the dark.  What am I?

     ANSWER: GRUE

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 8.  I am huge and white and tied up in a cave shaped like a head.
     A man in a purple costume owns me, and sometimes he lets others
     ride me.  What am I?

     ANSWER: STALLION

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 9.  I am spun, I am sticky, and I am the only thing that will hold
     a witch still.  What am I?

     ANSWER: WEB

----------------------------------------------------------------------
10.  The one-armed pirate cannot bear my voice, because I count out
     loud what is coming for him.  What am I?

     ANSWER: CLOCK

----------------------------------------------------------------------
11.  I am a captain, I am a pirate, I have one arm, and the shipyard
     is my home.  Beware me.  Who am I?

     ANSWER: HOOK

----------------------------------------------------------------------
12.  Without me the Fairy Princess dies of the poison that was meant
     for Peter Pan.  What am I?

     ANSWER: WAND

----------------------------------------------------------------------
13.  From outside I look like the head of a human being, and you go
     in through my mouth.  What am I?

     ANSWER: SKULL

----------------------------------------------------------------------
14.  I am a raging torrent with boulders in me, I am long and wide,
     I have many caves, and this whole valley is named for me.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: RIVER

----------------------------------------------------------------------
15.  There are five sets of me.  One set opens the front office and
     will do for the other two as well.  What am I?

     ANSWER: KEYS

----------------------------------------------------------------------
16.  A prince of fairies owned me, a king of fairies will pay for me,
     and while you wear me the fairy rings cannot touch you.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: CROWN

----------------------------------------------------------------------
17.  I am fastened shut in a padded room, and my title is written
     backwards.  What am I?

     ANSWER: BOOK

----------------------------------------------------------------------
18.  One of me follows you and turns violent at the sight of food.
     One of me is looking for a witch.  One of me is only a grin.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: CAT

----------------------------------------------------------------------
19.  I growl, I guard, and I will not let you by unless the Phantom
     has marked you as good.  What am I?

     ANSWER: WOLF

----------------------------------------------------------------------
20.  The old lady with the cart will give me to you red, and there is
     sleeping poison in me.  Johnny will thank you for me green.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: APPLE

----------------------------------------------------------------------
21.  I am folded from paper, I am loaded in a catapult on the cliff,
     and I am the only way across the water.  What am I?

     ANSWER: AIRPLANE

----------------------------------------------------------------------
22.  We hang in a hive, we come and go, and what we make is stolen by
     a field of flowers.  What are we?

     ANSWER: BEES

----------------------------------------------------------------------
23.  I spin the thing that traps the witch, and I frighten a girl off
     her tuffet.  What am I?

     ANSWER: SPIDER

----------------------------------------------------------------------
24.  Green, I breathe fire and light your torches and eat frogs and
     birds.  Yellow, I wander the caverns looking for Jackie-Paper.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: DRAGON

----------------------------------------------------------------------
25.  There are two of us to find, male and female, and Bugs wants us
     back at the farm before the crate arrives.  What are we?

     ANSWER: RABBITS

----------------------------------------------------------------------
26.  I fell off a truck on the way to the rabbit farm, and I cannot be
     delivered until the rabbits are found and the truck is recovered.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: CARROTS

----------------------------------------------------------------------
27.  I belong to a rental agency, bank robbers took me, and if the
     policeman catches you with me you will go to jail.  What am I?

     ANSWER: TRUCK

----------------------------------------------------------------------
28.  I am in the equipment bag.  I burn oil, and I need the pan that
     is next to me on the shelf.  What am I?

     ANSWER: LAMP

----------------------------------------------------------------------
29.  I come in a box in the equipment bag.  Without me the lamp is
     only a lamp.  What am I?

     ANSWER: MATCHES

----------------------------------------------------------------------
30.  The shop sells me for a good many points, and two ordinary
     bullets come with me.  The silver one you must find yourself.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: GUN

----------------------------------------------------------------------
31.  I am on the floor, the walls and the ceiling of a small cell.
     A reward is offered to whoever brings me back there.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: PADDING

----------------------------------------------------------------------
32.  You may enter me as a spectator or as a prisoner, and if you are
     the second you may not leave except to go to court.  What am I?

     ANSWER: JAIL

----------------------------------------------------------------------
33.  I sit behind the bench in a long dark robe with a gavel in my
     hand, and your lawyer must find me before you go free.
     Who am I?

     ANSWER: JUDGE

----------------------------------------------------------------------
34.  Answer the burly bartender enough times and he hands me over,
     and writes your name on the sign.  What am I?

     ANSWER: DIPLOMA

----------------------------------------------------------------------
35.  I am surrounded by more of myself, and the swamp is my neighbour.
     What am I?

     ANSWER: MARSH

----------------------------------------------------------------------
36.  I am a river, I am a valley, I am a room with a model in it, and
     in the wizard's diary I am the magic word.  What am I?

     ANSWER: AMAZON
