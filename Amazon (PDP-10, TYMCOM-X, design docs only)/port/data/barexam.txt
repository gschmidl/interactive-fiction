AMAZON -- the 36 Bar Exam questions (room 10)
=============================================

  ####  RECONSTRUCTED CONTENT -- NOT FROM THE TAPES  ####

Two lines in ROOMS (22-Sep-77) are all that survives of these tables:

    room  1:  "You must answer a riddle to leave (the way you came in)
               ....there are 36 different riddles."
    room 10:  "....*The bartender asks you a question.
               (There are 36 different questions)"

and AMAZON.TXT (09-Sep-79) gives the machinery that indexed them:

    (READ   (DISPLAY  RIDTXT(RIDDLE)))
    (ANSWER (EQUAL (RIDCOD(RIDDLE) INTEXT) ...))

RIDTXT and RIDCOD themselves were never written to any file that survives.
The 36 items below were written for the Windows port so that the room is
playable.  They are drawn only from AMAZON's own material -- every answer is
a room, object or creature named in ROOMS or OBJECT.TYP -- and nothing is
borrowed from any other game.

This file is the plain-text copy of the table in port/src/riddles.c.  If the
originals are ever recovered, replace both and nothing else has to change.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 1.  Counsellor.  Who is the only one here who can arrest a person
     and request a fine?

     ANSWER: POLICEMAN

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 2.  What must a prisoner have before he can get out of the Jail?

     ANSWER: LAWYER

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 3.  Whom must that lawyer find before the prisoner goes free?

     ANSWER: JUDGE

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 4.  Name the book that belongs to the lawyers and the police and is
     necessary to get out of Jail.

     ANSWER: LAWBOOK

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 5.  A prisoner may not leave the Jail except when taken where?

     ANSWER: COURT

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 6.  What garment does the figure behind the bench wear?

     ANSWER: ROBE

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 7.  What is in that figure's hand?

     ANSWER: GAVEL

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 8.  Besides the Bench and the Jury box, name one of the two other
     furnishings of the Court Room.

     ANSWER: DESK

----------------------------------------------------------------------
 9.  Where does a man go who has been found GUILTY by the court?

     ANSWER: EXECUTION

----------------------------------------------------------------------
10.  After an execution, when may the condemned and his cohorts come
     back into the valley?

     ANSWER: TOMORROW

----------------------------------------------------------------------
11.  If the policeman catches you with the stolen rental vehicle,
     where do you go?

     ANSWER: JAIL

----------------------------------------------------------------------
12.  Who took that vehicle from the rental agency?

     ANSWER: ROBBERS

----------------------------------------------------------------------
13.  What does the sign in this tavern read, before the list of
     names?  Give the first word.

     ANSWER: EXAMINERS

----------------------------------------------------------------------
14.  What document qualifies a person as a lawyer?

     ANSWER: DIPLOMA

----------------------------------------------------------------------
15.  How many different questions are in this examination?

     ANSWER: 36

----------------------------------------------------------------------
16.  Who serves the drinks in this establishment?

     ANSWER: TROLL

----------------------------------------------------------------------
17.  What is the court jester's name?

     ANSWER: TONTO

----------------------------------------------------------------------
18.  Who is that jester's friend, the one who drops silver bullets?

     ANSWER: RANGER

----------------------------------------------------------------------
19.  What is the only thing that will kill a vampire, apart from that
     same gentleman?

     ANSWER: SILVER

----------------------------------------------------------------------
20.  The Phantom gives out a token for helping him.  What is it
     called?  One word.

     ANSWER: GOOD-MARK

----------------------------------------------------------------------
21.  Who must have that token before he will let you pass?

     ANSWER: WOLF

----------------------------------------------------------------------
22.  What animal is the wicked witch allergic to being splashed with?

     ANSWER: WATER

----------------------------------------------------------------------
23.  What may a witch be tied up with?

     ANSWER: WEBS

----------------------------------------------------------------------
24.  Name the ruler of the jungle animals.

     ANSWER: TARZAN

----------------------------------------------------------------------
25.  If you kill one of his animals, what must you then do with it?

     ANSWER: EAT

----------------------------------------------------------------------
26.  Who lives in the tree-house and is expecting a child?

     ANSWER: JANE

----------------------------------------------------------------------
27.  The baby found floating in a basket has a name.  What is it?

     ANSWER: MOSES

----------------------------------------------------------------------
28.  Whose laboratory do the white mice belong in?

     ANSWER: LIVINGSTON

----------------------------------------------------------------------
29.  Who owns the wingless monkey?

     ANSWER: TARZAN

----------------------------------------------------------------------
30.  Who owns the winged one?

     ANSWER: WITCH

----------------------------------------------------------------------
31.  A boy rescues people from the pirate ship.  Where does he live?
     Answer with the name of the land.

     ANSWER: NEVER-NEVER

----------------------------------------------------------------------
32.  What two things is Captain Hook afraid of?  Name either one.

     ANSWER: CLOCKS

----------------------------------------------------------------------
33.  Within what circle is a wizard powerless?  Two words, run
     together, or just the second.

     ANSWER: RING

----------------------------------------------------------------------
34.  What is written on the cake?

     ANSWER: EAT-ME

----------------------------------------------------------------------
35.  What is written on the urn?

     ANSWER: DRINK-ME

----------------------------------------------------------------------
36.  Last question, counsellor.  The wizard's diary gives a magic
     word.  What is it?

     ANSWER: AMAZON
