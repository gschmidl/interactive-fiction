# Prime-50 games

- [Ankh (Prime 50-Series, PRIMOS Pascal)](Ankh%20%28Prime%2050-Series%2C%20PRIMOS%20Pascal%29/) - Randall Tice's treasure hunt (1984), with a myna bird that gives hints and a gremlin that picks pockets.
- [Dungeon by Jonathan Reed (Prime 50-Series, PL1G, full source)](Dungeon%20by%20Jonathan%20Reed%20%28Prime%2050-Series%2C%20PL1G%2C%20full%20source%29/) - Jonathan H. Reed's DUNGEON (1983), a dragon crawl through a 10 x 10 x 10 dungeon.
- [Tower (Prime 50-Series, PRIMOS Pascal)](Tower%20%28Prime%2050-Series%2C%20PRIMOS%20Pascal%29/) - Randall Tice's castle-and-caverns adventure (1984) of fifty-nine rooms.
