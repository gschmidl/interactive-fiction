# Tower (Randall Tice, 1984)

*Tower* (program CHIP1), a castle-and-caverns adventure in PRIMOS Pascal, "courtesy of R. L. Tice":
fifty-nine rooms with a moated castle, a forest of climbable trees, a belfry with a vampire bat, a
torture chamber, a Troglodyte king, a Tyrannosaurus and a gypsy who buys your treasures and steals
them back. Words may be abbreviated to three letters. Native Windows port of the Pascal, with its map
and vocabulary files recovered from a PRIMOS disk built in.

## Command line

`tower.exe` takes no parameters.

## Recommended start

`tower.exe`
