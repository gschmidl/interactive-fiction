# Ankh (Randall Tice, 1984)

*Ankh* (program ANKH1), a treasure hunt in PRIMOS Pascal written by Randall Tice at the College of
William & Mary in January 1984: six treasures, twenty-four rooms, a myna bird that gives hints, a
gremlin that picks your pocket, and a door marked south that kills you if you open it. Words may be
abbreviated to three letters. Native Windows port of the Pascal, with its map and vocabulary files
recovered from a PRIMOS disk built in.

## Command line

`ankh.exe` takes no parameters.

## Recommended start

`ankh.exe`
