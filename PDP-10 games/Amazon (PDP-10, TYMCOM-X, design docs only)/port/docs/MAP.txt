21 rooms.  Every exit below is reconstructed -- see RECONSTRUCTION.md 3.2 for
which ones have a textual basis and which are free invention.

Rooms 8 and 9 are on the far bank of the Amazon and are reached ONLY by
LAUNCHing the paper airplane from the Top of Cliff.  Take the parachute or
the life-raft: "Nice to take a parachute, life-raft in case you miss".

Rooms 1, 2, 16 and 20 are the cave system and have no daylight.  You need
the flashlight (with batteries) or the oil lamp (with the pan of oil and the
matches) before you go down, or the Grue will have you.
