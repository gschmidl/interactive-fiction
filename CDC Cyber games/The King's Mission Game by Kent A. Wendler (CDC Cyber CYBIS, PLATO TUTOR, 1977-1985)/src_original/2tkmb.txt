        are moving at any moment.  Your bearing
        shows the direction in which you are
        moving.  This direction is given by a
        number similar to the numbers on the
        face of a clock.  For instance, a bearing
        of 3 shows a direction of due east.  Due
        south is a bearing of 6.  A bearing of
        10.5 is to the northwest.  Due north
        can be given as either 12 or 0, which
        are the same bearing.
        
          Your speed, which can be any whole
        number from 0 to 3, shows how far you
        can move at a time if you are moving
        either due north or south, or east or
        west.  If you are moving in a diagonal
        direction, you will move a slightly
        different amount--to the position nearest
        the whole-numbered position.
        
          You will find that you cannot go from
        a stop to full speed (or from full speed

        traveling at full speed, that you can-
