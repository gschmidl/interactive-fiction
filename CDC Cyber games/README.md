# CDC Cyber games

- [Adventure by Eric Pepke (CDC Cyber, PLATO TUTOR, 1980)](Adventure%20by%20Eric%20Pepke%20%28CDC%20Cyber%2C%20PLATO%20TUTOR%2C%201980%29/) - *Ninnies and Cretins*, a 48-room adventure with a sentence parser from Florida State's PLATO system.
- [Avatar by Bruce Maggs, Andrew Shapira and David Sides (CDC Cyber CYBIS, PLATO TUTOR, 1979-1994)](Avatar%20by%20Bruce%20Maggs%2C%20Andrew%20Shapira%20and%20David%20Sides%20%28CDC%20Cyber%20CYBIS%2C%20PLATO%20TUTOR%2C%201979-1994%29/) - The largest of the PLATO dungeon games: a city of shops and guilds above fifteen dungeon levels.
- [Hobbits by Michael Jackson (CDC Cyber, PLATO TUTOR, 1980)](Hobbits%20by%20Michael%20Jackson%20%28CDC%20Cyber%2C%20PLATO%20TUTOR%2C%201980%29/) - Class work whose riddle game has Gollum ask Bilbo five of Tolkien's riddles from *The Hobbit*.
- [Middle-earth by Thomas A. Wooded (CDC Cyber, PLATO TUTOR, 1980, unfinished)](Middle-earth%20by%20Thomas%20A.%20Wooded%20%28CDC%20Cyber%2C%20PLATO%20TUTOR%2C%201980%2C%20unfinished%29/) - The start of a Tolkien travel-and-adventure game, with the arcade game *Space Invasion* beside it.
- [Moria by Kevet Duncombe and Jim Battin (CDC Cyber CYBIS, PLATO TUTOR, 1978-1993)](Moria%20by%20Kevet%20Duncombe%20and%20Jim%20Battin%20%28CDC%20Cyber%20CYBIS%2C%20PLATO%20TUTOR%2C%201978-1993%29/) - The great PLATO dungeon game: sixty levels of first-person 3D corridors under a city, with guilds and spells.
- [Quest by Gary Stollman (CDC Cyber, PLATO TUTOR, 1980, unfinished)](Quest%20by%20Gary%20Stollman%20%28CDC%20Cyber%2C%20PLATO%20TUTOR%2C%201980%2C%20unfinished%29/) - A student's *Adventure*-style game, never finished.
- [The King's Mission Game by Kent A. Wendler (CDC Cyber CYBIS, PLATO TUTOR, 1977-1985)](The%20King's%20Mission%20Game%20by%20Kent%20A.%20Wendler%20%28CDC%20Cyber%20CYBIS%2C%20PLATO%20TUTOR%2C%201977-1985%29/) - The King sends you to clear his enchanted realms of goblins, zombies, werewolves and demons.
- [The Labyrinth by Michael Wei (CDC Cyber CYBIS, PLATO TUTOR, 1980-1996)](The%20Labyrinth%20by%20Michael%20Wei%20%28CDC%20Cyber%20CYBIS%2C%20PLATO%20TUTOR%2C%201980-1996%29/) - "A constantly evolving dungeon game": a castle, shops and a casino above a line-drawn 3D dungeon.
