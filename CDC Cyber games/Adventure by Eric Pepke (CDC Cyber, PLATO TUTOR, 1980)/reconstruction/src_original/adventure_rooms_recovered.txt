Eric Pepke, "Ninnies and Cretins: a variation on Adventure" (FSU PLATO, 1979-80)
Recovered rooms and map
=====================================================================================

Where this comes from
  The lesson on the packs (v2.2, edited 05/26/80) loads its rooms and map from
  dataset ADVENTDS in master file CMAST. CMAST is empty both on the NOS 1.3
  packs and on the plato4nos13 PFDUMP tape they were restored from (it holds
  only GUESTNOTES, 08/12/89), so ADVENTDS itself is gone. An earlier version
  kept the rooms in the source as two WRITEC lists and the map in the lesson's
  common, and both survive inside the lesson file:
    short names   unused block 16(8)               all 48 rooms
    long 29-48    unused block 15(8)               start of 29 lost
    long 2-10     slack after block 'describe'     start of 2 lost
    map           common 'info', nc1-nc96          all 48 rooms
  The tape copy and the disk copy of the lesson are word-for-word identical.
  (On the disk the lesson crosses a NOS track boundary at logical sector 108.)

Numbering
  WRITEC LOC,(neg),(zero),1,2,... so the first entry is room 1. It agrees with
  the code: 'climb stalagmite' sets 41; the rubble moves set 15/16 and 46/47;
  the lit-room test (loc<11, 11 with doors open, 13, 27, 29-34, 36) hits the
  outdoor rooms, the tower, the shaft-lit rooms, the chasm tunnels, the airy
  room and the balcony. The map agrees with every surviving description.

Map format (the old in-common version)
  2 words per room, 6 fields of 9 bits per word (the low 6 bits are always 0):
  n s e w u d in out, then what magic words 1-4 do in that room (the spells in
  parchment order: opening, closing, transmutation, power).
  1-255 = move to that room; 3xx = refusal message; 4xx = special unit.
  The dataset version keeps the same 12 fields as 10-bit segments.

Still lost: the long descriptions of room 1 and rooms 11-28, and the start of
rooms 2 and 29.
