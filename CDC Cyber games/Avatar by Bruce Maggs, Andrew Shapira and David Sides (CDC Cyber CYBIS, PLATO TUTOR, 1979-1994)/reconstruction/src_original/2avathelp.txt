=== block 'read me'  kind 47  101 words  (block 1)
Comments concerning the adding of a new page:
 
1. Check -vocabs- to make sure words required by -concept-
are present.
 
2. Add -concept- on the end.  Please document every 5th
-concept- to make additions easier.  Unit "concept".
 
3. Add a tag to the -jump- table in unit "jumptab".
 
4. Add a description of the page and the keyword to the
HELP section (unit(s) "flist1", "flist2" ...)
 
4. At the beginning of every page that can be gotten to
by a -concept- put a -do- to unit "setinfo" with the keyword
as the parameter:
 
do      setinfo('races')        $$ for example
 
SHIFT-LAB will display the current keyword.
It uses line 31, so don't display anything on line 31.
 
Remember, the following are in the -imain-:
 
NEXT1   return to game
LAB1    show current keyword
DATA    what feature arrow

        Avatar is the result of many years of work and play by
        literally hundreds of people.  We hope that you enjoy
        playing the game as much as we have enjoyed creating it.
 
        The game offers fun to the beginner and has enough
        complexity to keep the expert challenged.  One of the
        most important aspects of the game is the interaction
        between the players.  The players make this game.
 
        The game is based on many "dungeon games" that have
        come before it and adds many new ideas.  There is no
        absolute goal to the game; the players generally make
        their own goals.  You will probably pick and change
        goals throughout your lifetime in the game.
 
        The basic concept behind the game is "role-playing".
        You will assume the role of a person (not necessarily
        human!) living in an "alternate reality" where the
        impossible is possible.  Magic exists in Avatar.
 
        The game may appear complex to the beginner.  Don't be
        concerned, just dig in and enjoy.  As with the real
        world you will only need to know a small number of
        rules to get started.  Good luck!

        DATA will always return you to the page where you

        Your pseudonym or name you wish to be known by is used
        whenever someone in the game wishes to refer to you
        specifically.  For example, when giving you gold or

        and dexterous you can be.  There are several other
        important points concerning your race; they will be
        discussed later.

        Goblin's chance of hitting Mithir = (50+6-9)  = 47%

        There are several other modifications such as whether
        you are invisible and whether the monster can see
