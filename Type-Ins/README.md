# Type-Ins

- [ASCII-Ladden (C64)](ASCII-Ladden%20%28C64%29/) - *ASCII-ladden*, a Norwegian adventure typed in from *Mikrodata* (1985).
- [Burglar House (NEC PC-8001, type-in)](Burglar%20House%20%28NEC%20PC-8001%2C%20type-in%29/) - *Burglar House* (1983), a Japanese machine-code adventure: burgle a mansion between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- [House Adventure (Fujitsu FM-7, type-in)](House%20Adventure%20%28Fujitsu%20FM-7%2C%20type-in%29/) - *House Adventure* 1.2 (1983), a Japanese graphic adventure typed in from *My Computer*.
- [House Adventure (NEC PC-8001mkII, type-in)](House%20Adventure%20%28NEC%20PC-8001mkII%2C%20type-in%29/) - *House Adventure* 1.2 by PC Penguins (1983), a Japanese graphic adventure typed in from *My Computer*.
- [Il mistero della Montagna d'Argento (BBC Micro, Italian type-in)](Il%20mistero%20della%20Montagna%20d'Argento%20%28BBC%20Micro%2C%20Italian%20type-in%29/) - The Italian edition of Usborne's *The Mystery of the Silver Mountain*, as a BBC Micro disc.
- [Iroha Uta no Nazo (MZ-80B)](Iroha%20Uta%20no%20Nazo%20%28MZ-80B%29/) - *Iroha Uta no Nazo* (1983), a Japanese adventure typed in from *Oh!MZ*.
- [M no Higeki (Sharp MZ-2200, type-in)](M%20no%20Higeki%20%28Sharp%20MZ-2200%2C%20type-in%29/) - *M no Higeki* ("The Tragedy of M"), a Japanese mystery adventure with graphics.
- [Memo Satsujin (Sharp MZ-80K, type-in)](Memo%20Satsujin%20%28Sharp%20MZ-80K%2C%20type-in%29/) - *Memo Satsujin* ("Memo Murder"), a Japanese graphic adventure: find the memo that names the killer.
- [Mystery House T.S2 (Sharp MZ-80K, type-in)](Mystery%20House%20T.S2%20%28Sharp%20MZ-80K%2C%20type-in%29/) - The *Mystery House T.S* sequel (1983): find the three diamonds hidden in the house and get out.
- [Opération Sescuballi (ZX81)](Op%C3%A9ration%20Sescuballi%20%28ZX81%29/) - A French adventure from *Casus Belli* (1982): recover the microfilms before the guards come at 22:10.
- [The Sppy (NEC PC-6001, type-in)](The%20Sppy%20%28NEC%20PC-6001%2C%20type-in%29/) - *The Sppy*, a Japanese spy adventure, as cassette recordings.
