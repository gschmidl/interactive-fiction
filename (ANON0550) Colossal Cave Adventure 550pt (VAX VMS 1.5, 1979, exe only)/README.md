# ADVENT.EXE on the VAX/VMS V1.5 binary distribution tape (June 1979)

**Status: STAGED 2026-09-19 - not ported, nothing built.** Files here are verified copies (md5) of the originals named below.

Source: `F:\bits\DEC\vax\vms\VMS_1.x\BB-D782B-BE_VMS_1.5_bin_jun79.tap.gz` (the `.zip` beside it is the same tape).

`DB1:[GOLDEN]ADVENT.EXE;1` (name at ~5376573 of the unzipped tape; text at ~5407000). The text is Platt's 550-point cave
("small chamber filled with large boulders. The walls are very warm", "Caution! Bear in room!", Valley of the Stone Faces
family) - i.e. a 550 build already on a DEC-internal VMS kit in mid-1979. Executable only.
Runs on our VAX/VMS image runner (see reference_vax_vms_emulation) once cut out of the save set; VMS 1.5 images are
pre-V2 format, so the image activator needs checking.
