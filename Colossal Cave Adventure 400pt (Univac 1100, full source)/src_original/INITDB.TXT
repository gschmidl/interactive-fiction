.
. This is an assembly language routine written in Univac 1100 series
. MASM (Meta Assembler) language.  This routine initializes the D-bank.
.
. First, we check to see if there is a file named <user id>*<filename>
. in existance.  If so, we tell the user about it, and ask him if he wants
. to recover the previous game.  If he does, we read it into the D-bank.
. If the user's file doesn't exist, or he doesnt't want to recover the old
. game, the mast file '<qualifier>*<filename>' is assigned and read into
. the D-bank.  If that file does not exist, then the program should have the
. '8' file assigned.
.
. These files are all word addressable drum (either actual or simulated).
. (I have found no need for this.  They're now sector-addressable - DK)
.
. These routines were written by Elliot S. Itkin of
. the University of Maryland CSC Systems staff, and were
. converted to upper/lower case ASCII and MASM by Duff Kurland,
. of Information Systems Design, Inc.
.
. A HANDY PROC FIRST
. THIS PROC DOES AN INFO$ FOR US.
. IT IS CALLED:
.
.       I$NFO  <FUNC>,<WORDS NEEDED TO HOLD INFO>,<PLACE TO PUT INFO>
.
.
P         PROC                        .
I$NFO*    NAME                        .
          J         THERE             . SKIP CREATION OF THE TABLE
TABLE     +         P(1,1),0,0        . FIRST WORD OF INFO$ PACKET TABLE
          +         P(1,2),P(1,3)     . SECOND WORD OF TABLE
PKT       +         2,TABLE           . THE PACKET THAT IS PUT INTO A0
THERE     L         A0,PKT            . GET PACKET
          ER        INFO$             . DO IT
          TZ,S4     TABLE             . IS STATUS ZERO?
          ER        ERR$              . NO, ERROR OFF
          END                         . OF PROC I$NFO
INITDB*.
          S         X11,SAVX11        . FIRST SAVE X11
          I$NFO     USRID$,2,ACCTNR   . GET USER ID (ACCT NR AT UOM)
          er        opt$              . get @XQT options
          tep,u     a0,1*/('z'-'i')   . is the I option set?
          j         return            . yes, read data from file 8
.
. CREATE THE @ASG,A IMAGE OF '@ASG,A <USER ID>*<FILENAME>'
.
          E$DIT     EDPKT             . OPEN EDITING
          E$MSG     ASGM              . START ASG MESSAGE
          E$FD2     ACCTNR            . INSERT ACCOUNT NUMBER
          E$MSGR                      . CONTINUE TO END OF @ASG IMAGE
          E$DITX                      . END EDITING
          L,U       A0,LINE           . PREP FOR CSF$
          ER        CSF$              . '@ASG,A <USER ID>*<FILENAME> . '
          JN        A0,INIT10         . WE DIDN'T GET THE FILE, TRY MASTER FILE
.
. WE GOT THE FILE. PUT USE NAME ON IT AND CHECK TO SEE IF CURRENT
. ABSOLUTE EXECUTED IT.
. IF OKAY, ASK IF HE WANTS TO RECOVER IT.
. THE FORMAT OF THE FILE IS A WORD ADDRESSABLE DRUM WITH
. THE FIRST WORD BEING AN ABSOLUTE ID AND
. THE SECOND WORD BEING A TDATE$ WORD OF THE TIME IT WAS SAVED
. AND AFTER THAT THE REST IS A DBANK FOR THE PROGRAM.
.
          L         A0,('@USE $')     .
          S         A0,LINE           . PUT USE NAME OF $ ON FILE
          L,U       A0,','            . GET A COMMA
          S,S1      A0,LINE+1         . PUT AWAY
          L,U       A0,LINE           . GET @USE IMAGE
          ER        CSF$              . AND DO IT
.
. READ THE FIRST 3 WORDS OF THE FILE.  TELL HIM WE'VE FOUND IT AND
. FIND OUT IF IT IS OKAY.
. ASK HIM IF HE WANTS TO RECOVER.
.
          I$OW      FIRSTPKT          . READ FIRST 3 WORDS OF FILE
          tz,s1     firstpkt+3        . did that go ok?
          er        eabt$             . no
          L         A0,AID            . GET ABSOLUTE ID
          TE        A0,FIRST          . IS IT THE SAME?
          J         INIT05            . NO GOOD, TELL HIM AND TRY MASTER FILE
.
. AT THIS POINT THE FILE IS OKAY, ASK ABOUT RECOVERY.
.
          a$edit    aedpkt            . OPEN EDITING
          a$emsg    MSG               . INSERT THE MSG
          a$eday3   SECOND            . INSERT THE DAY PORTION
          a$emsgr                     . CONTINUE
          a$etime   SECOND            . INSERT THE TIME PORTION
          a$emsgr                     . FINISH UP THE MESSAGE
          a$editx                     . END EDITING
INIT01    a$print   aedpcw            . PRINT THE MESSAGE
          a$print   QUEST             . ASK HIM IF HE WANTS TO RECOVER
          L         A0,(EOF,BUF)      . GET ACW FOR READ$
          ER        READ$             . READ RESPONSE
          L,S1      A0,BUF            . GET FIRST LETTER OF RESPONSE
          TNE,U     A0,'Y'            . WAS IT A YES?
          J         YES               . YES IT WAS
          TNE,U     A0,'N'            . WAS IT A NO?
          J         NO                . YES IT WAS
          J         INIT01            . IT WASN'T EITHER, TRY AGAIN
.
. THE FILE THAT WAS SAVED HAS HAD THE ABSOLUTE CHANGED FROM UNDER IT.
. TELL HIM WE CAN'T RECOVER AND TRY MASTER FILE
.
INIT05.
          a$print   CANTREC           . TELL HIM WE CAN'T RECOVER
          J         NO                . TREAT AS A NO TO THE QUERY
.
. HE SAID YES, RESTORE THIS GAME
.
YES.
          lxi,u     a0,4              . load D-bank BDI
          lxm,h1    a0,third          . load required upper limit
          er        mcore$            . expand D-bank
          l,h1      a0,third          . get last D-bank address
          an,h2     a0,third          . calculate size of D-bank
          a,u       a0,1              .
          s,h1      a0,dbankpkt+4     . set in I/O packet
          l,h2      a0,third          . get D-bank start address
          s,h2      a0,dbankpkt+4     . set in I/O packet
          I$OW      DBANKPKT          . INITIALIZE THE DBANK
          tz,s1     dbankpkt+3        . did that go ok?
          er        eabt$             . no
          L,U       A0,FREFIL         . FREE THE FILE AND DELETE IT
          ER        CSF$              . '@FREE $ . '
          J         RETURN            . TO CALLER
.
. HE SAID NO, FREE THE FILE AND TRY THE MASTER FILE
.
NO.
.
. FIND OUT FIRST IF HE WANTS THE FILE DELETED OR NOT
.
          ascii
          L,U       A0,TREADPKT       . GET TREAD PKT FOR QUESTION
          ER        atread$           . DO A TREAD TO FIND OUT
          L,U       A0,,A0            . ZERO UPPER A0
          JZ        A0,NO             . IF NO RESPONSE, TRY AGAIN.
          L         A0,BUF            . GET FIRST LETTER OF RESPONSE
          ssl       a0,9*3            .
          te,u      a0,'y'            .
          TNE,U     A0,'Y'            . DID HE SAY 'YES', DELETE IT?
          J         NO001             . YES HE DID
          te,u      a0,'n'            .
          TNE,U     A0,'N'            . DID HE SAY 'NO', DON'T DELETE IT?
          J         NO002             . HE SAID NO, DON'T DELETE IT.
          J         NO                . HE SAID NEITHER, TRY AGAIN
          fieldata
.
. COME HERE TO DELETE FILE
.
NO001.
          L,U       A0,FRDFIL         . DELETE FILE
          J         NO003             . FINISH UP
.
. COME HERE IF WE DON'T DELETE FILE
.
NO002.
          L,U       A0,FREFIL         . DON'T DELETE FILE, JUST FREE IT.
          J         NO003             . FINISH UP
.
. NOW COME HERE, FREE THE FILE AND FINISH UP
.
NO003.
          ER        CSF$              . '@FREE[,D] $ . '
          J         INIT10            . USE THE MASTER FILE
.
. ATTEMPT TO ASSIGN THE MASTER FILE.  IF SUCCESSFUL COPY IT INTO
. THE DBANK, IF NOT JUST RETURN AND HE BETTER HAVE FILE '8'
. ASSIGNED.
.
INIT10.
          L,U       A0,MASG           .
          ER        CSF$              . ATTEMPT TO ASSIGN IT
          JN        A0,RETURN         . WE DIDN'T GET IT, USE FILE '8'
.
. WE GOT THE MASTER FILE, PUT A USE NAME OF '$' ON IT
.
          L         A0,('@USE $')     .
          S         A0,MASG           . PUT AWAY
          L,U       A0,','            .
          S,S1      A0,MASG+1         . PUT AWAY THE COMMA
          L,U       A0,MASG           .
          ER        CSF$              . '@USE $,<QUALIFIER>*<FILENAME>
          I$OW      FIRSTPKT          . READ FIRST TO CHECK AID OF FILE
          tz,s1     firstpkt+3        . did that go ok?
          er        eabt$             . no
          L         A0,AID            . GET ABSOLUTE ID
          TE        A0,FIRST          . IS IT THE SAME?
          J         INIT15            . NO, JUST FREE FILE AND RETURN
          lxi,u     a0,4              . load D-bank BDI
          lxm,h1    a0,third          . load required upper limit
          er        mcore$            . expand D-bank
          l,h1      a0,third          . get last D-bank address
          an,h2     a0,third          . calculate size of D-bank
          a,u       a0,1              .
          s,h1      a0,dbankpkt+4     . set in I/O packet
          l,h2      a0,third          . get D-bank start address
          s,h2      a0,dbankpkt+4     . set in I/O packet
          I$OW      DBANKPKT          . READ IT IN
          tz,s1     dbankpkt+3        . did that go ok?
          er        eabt$             . no
INIT15    L,U       A0,FREFIL         . ALL FINISHED, FREE IT
          ER        CSF$              .
          J         RETURN            .
.
. RETURN TO FORTRAN
.
RETURN.
          L         X11,SAVX11        . RESTORE X11
          tz,s1     0,x11             . called from ASCII FORTRAN (FTN) ?
          j         returnftn         . yes
          J         1,X11             . no, return to FORTRAN-V
returnftn .
          j         0,x11             return to FTN program
