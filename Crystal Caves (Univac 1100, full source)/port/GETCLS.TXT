.         PRIVIL  --  Check for privileged adventurer
.
.         This subroutine of one argument checks to see if the
.         current user is one of "the privileged few" who can be a
.         wizard by answering everything wrong!  It returns a LOGICAL
.         value indicating whether this user is permitted to be a
.         wizard with wrong answers.
.
.         The calling sequence is:
.
.         LOGICAL WRONG
.         CALL PRIVIL(WRONG)
.
          axr$
$(1)      .
privil*   .
          sa        a0,a2               save argument list address
          dl        a0,acct             get account number from GETCLS
          lr,u      r1,tablel           get table length
          lx        x10,(2,-2)          initialize index
search    .
          se        a0,table+2,*x10     is he a good guy?
          j         nogood              no
          te        a1,table+1,x10      maybe.  check 2nd word
          j         search              nope.  keep searching
          la,u      a0,1                good guy:  get a .TRUE. value
          j         return              return to caller
nogood    .
          la,u      a0,0                get a .FALSE. value
return    .
          l         a1,0,a2             get address of first argument
          s         a0,0,a1             set logical value
          j         0,x11               return to caller
.
$(0)      .
table     .                             table of good guys
          '0001SYDUFF'
tablel    equ       ($-table)/2
