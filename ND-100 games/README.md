# ND-100 games

- [Adventure ENB (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](Adventure%20ENB%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - A Swedish princess quest in ND BASIC (1983) on a land that is new every game.
- [Cave Fun (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](Cave%20Fun%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - Mikael Johansson's 1981 adventure: a fortress behind an iron door with 27 treasures to bring home.
- [DOD (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](DOD%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - A short Swedish dungeon game (1983): lead your company of fifteen out of the caves of evil.
- [Legend (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](Legend%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - LEGEND v10.0 (1984-87), a Swedish multi-player fantasy game with the club's own worlds.
- [Mordor (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](Mordor%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - *Mordor: Land of evil* (1985): carry the Ring through Middle Earth to Mount Doom.
- [My World (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](My%20World%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - Mikael Johansson's MY_WORLD (1983): a mountain, a troll's bridge and a pirate's maze of tunnels.
