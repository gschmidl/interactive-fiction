1
YOU ARE IN A ROOM DESIGNED TO RESEMBLE A VAST ENTRY CHAMBER.  THE AIR
HERE IS COLD AND DAMP.  PASSAGES LEAD NORTH, WEST AND EAST.
2
YOU ARE NEAR WHAT SEEMS TO BE THE MARTIAN EQUIVALENT OF A POST-CARD
STAND.  RACKS ON EITHER SIDE CONTAIN STIFF PAPER-BACKED PICTURES OF
MARS AS SEEN FROM NEAR ORBIT.
3
YOU ARE IN A LOW CYLINDRICAL ROOM WITH DOORWAYS TO THE WEST, NORTH AND
EAST.  OVER EACH DOOR IS A BOLDLY PRINTED SIGN IN LOW MIDDLE
MARTIAN READING, "MAKWU".
4
YOU ARE IN THE HALL OF THE OLD ONES.  DESPITE THE LACK OF FURNISHINGS,
THE CHAMBER FEELS LIVED-IN.  THE GREAT LIFE FORCE ITSELF SEEMS TO
POKE AT YOUR MIND, YET YOUR COMPUTER REGISTERS NO LIFE SIGNATURES BUT
YOUR OWN.  THERE ARE CAVERNOUS PASSAGES LEADING TO THE NORTH, EAST AND
SOUTH.
BUT YOUR COMPUTER REGISTERS NOTHING AND THERE IS NO SOUND.  THERE ARE PASSAGES
TO THE EAST, NORTH AND SOUTH.
5
YOU ARE IN A ROOM WHICH IS REMINISCENT OF A HUMAN TOURIST TRAP.  THERE ARE
THINGS THAT LOOK LIKE SOUVENIRS HANGING ON STRINGS, MORE POSTCARDS, AND SMALL
IMITATIONS OF OTHER MARTIAN ARTIFACTS.  THE MERCHANDISE APPEARS TO BE PROTECTED
BY A SECURITY FIELD .  THE ONLY WAY OUT IS SOUTH.
6
YOU ARE IN A MASSIVE LOOPING CORRIDOR AT THE WESTERN END OF WHAT LOOKS LIKE
AN OFFICE.  PASSAGES ARE TO THE SOUTH AND THE WEST.
7
YOU ARE IN THE OFFICES OF MODERN MARTIAN MAGAZINE, THE MARVELOUS MAG THAT
BRINGS YOU THE MOSTEST ABOUT MANY THINGS ON MARS.  BEHIND LOCKED CABINETS
ARE OLD STACKED COPIES OF "MODERN MARTIAN".  AROUND ONE OF THE MACHINES THERE
IS A THICK PILE OF DUST, THE ACCUMULATION OF A LONG TIME.  A HIGH SPEED LASER
PRINTING PRESS IS BOLTED TO THE FLOOR.  THERE ARE DOORS TO THE WEST, NORTH,
EAST AND SOUTH.
8
YOU ARE IN A SPHERICAL ROOM WITH LIGHTED WALLS.  IN THE CENTRE THERE IS A
LARGE PLANT PEDESTAL.  THE ONLY WAY OUT IS BACK NORTH
9
YOU ARE AT THE EASTERN END OF A MASSIVE LOOPING CORRIDOR.  THERE APPEAR TO BE
OFFICES TO THE WEST, AND A PASSAGE LEADS NORTH.  IT IS ODDLY CHILL HERE.
10
YOU ARE IN AN UPWARD SLOPING CORRIDOR, AT THE BOTTOM.  THERE IS A DOOR LEADING
SOUTH
11
YOU ARE AT THE TOP OF A LONG DOWN SLOPING CORRIDOR.  THERE ARE DOORS TO THE
WEST AND TO THE NORTH.
12
YOU ARE IN A ROOM WITH FOUR MASSIVE MARBLE PILLARS STRETCHING FROM THE FLOOR
TO THE CEILING, OVER 1000 FEET ABOVE YOUR HEAD.  GLOWING SOFTLY IN THE CENTRE
OF THE PILLARS IS THE ORANGE LIGHT OF A HELIUM NEON LASER BEAM WHICH COMES UP
VIA A PINHOLE IN THE FLOOR.  THE ONLY PASSAGE IS BACK EAST
13
YOU ARE IN A VERY SMALL ROOM WITH A PASAGE LEADING EAST AND DOWN.  TO THE
NORTHEAST THERE IS A GLIMMERING RECTANGLE WITH A LARGE MACHINE CONNECTED TO IT.
THE MACHINE IS REPEATING OVER AND OVER, "PLACA PLACA PLACA PLACA..."
14
YOU ARE IN A SMALL TUBULAR PASSAGE THAT LEADS FURTHER DOWN.  THE WAY IS STILL
DOWN
15
YOU ARE AT THE BOTTOM OF A LONG TUBULAR PASSAGE.  HERE, BLOWN OFF ITS HINGES
IS A LARGE METAL PRESSURE DOOR.  OFF TO THE WEST IS A DIMLY LIT ROOM
16
YOU ARE IN THE DIMLY LIT ROOM.  ON WONE WALL IS AN IMMENSE PICTURE WINDOW
WHERE THERE IS A BREATHTAKING SIGHT.  OUT OF THE WINDOW ARE THE STARS, AND
BELOW YOU IS THE VAST ARID SURFACE OF THE RED PLANET MARS.  THE WINDOW IS VERY
COLD AS THERE IS NO SUNLIGHT SHINING ON IT NOW.  OUTSIDE TO THE WEST IS A
FLATTENED LANDING PAD FOR MARTIAN SPACECRAFT WHICH COULD BE HANDLED BY YOUR SHIP
THERE IS NO OTHER CONCLUSION TO BE MADE BUT THAT YOU ARE ON THE MARTIAN MOON
DIEMOS, THE MOON OF TERROR.  THERE IS A PASSAGE EAST AND A LARGE AIRLOCK DOOR
ON THE NORTH WALL
17
YOU ARE INSIDE THE AIRLOCK ON DIEMOS.  THERE ARE SOUTH AND WEST DOORS
18
YOU ARE OUT ON THE FLAT LANDING FIELD BY THE DIEMOSIAN AIRLOCK.  THE VIEW OF THE
STARS IS BREATHTAKING, AND YOU PAUSE FOR A BREATH.  MOTHER EARTH CAN BE SEEN 
GLOWING SOUTHWARD
19
YOU ARE IN A LARGE MARTIAN ALCOVE, WITH PASSAGES NORTH, EAST AND DOWN
20
YOU ARE IN A GREAT ROOM WHICH IS NOT ONLY CIRCULAR, BUT HAS CURVING PASSAGES
WHICH LEAD AWAY TO THE NORTH AND SOUTH.  THERE ARE LARGE CIRCLES PAINTED ON
THE WALL, MUCH LIKE THE SYMBOL FOR THE ANCIENT 20TH CENTURY OLYMPICS BACK ON
EARTH
21
YOU ARE IN THE MIDDLE OF A GREAT CIRCULAR PASSAGE.  IT LEADS OFF TO THE NORTH 
AND SOUTH
22
YOU ARE IN THE EMPTY ROOM.  THERE IS NOTHING HERE.  PASSAGES ARE UP AND SOUTH
23
YOU ARE IN THE ROOM OF THE GREAT ANCIENT MARTIAN KING MWA.  MWA WAS CERTAINLY
GREAT AND THE WALLS ARE LAVISHLY DECORATED.  PASSAGES ARE NORTH AND SOUTH
24
YOU ARE AT THE GREAT JUNCTION OF MWA'S CORRIDORS.  HERE AN EAST-WEST CORRIDOR
HAS A NORTHEAST BRANCH THAT GOES ON FOR A GREAT DISTANCE
25
YOU ARE AT MWA'S END.  A SIGN ON THE WALL IN OLD HIGH MARTIAN SAYS THAT BEYOND
HERE IS WHERE MWA MET HIS DISMAL END.  THE WAY IS BLOCKED TO PROTECT THE
FOOLHARDY FROM VENTURING INTO AN AREA THAT WAS TOO MUCH EVEN FOR A KING.  THE
ONLY WAY OUT IS SOUTH
26
YOU ARE AT THE GREAT JUNCTION OF ACCESS.  PASSAGES LEAD IN ALL FOUR DIRECTIONS
OF THE COMPASS.  TO THE WEST THERE IS CLANKING.  OVER THE WESTERN DOOR IS A
SIGN IN OLD MIDDLE MARTIAN WHICH READS "BE WARNED"
27
YOU ARE IN THE UTILITY ROOM.  THE WALLS ARE LINED WITH RACKS AND SHELVES,
SOME OCCUPIED BY MYSTERIOUS TOOLS AND MACHINERY, SOME EMPTY. THERE ARE
WIDE PASSAGES TO THE EAST AND NORTH, AND AN IRIS DOOR TO THE WEST.
A SIGN OVER THE ENTRANCE TO THE NORTHERN PASSAGE, IN OLD HIGH MARTIAN,
READS, "BE FURTHER WARNED!"
28
THIS IS THE SECONDARY ROBOT ROOM. YOU WERE WARNED!!
(THERE IS A TUNNEL LEADING OUT TO THE SOUTH)
29
YOU ARE AT GMOG'S WAY, A CLOSE HALL WITH PLUSH CARPETING AND
RED VELVET WALL HANGINGS.  THE MAIN ROUTE LEADS NORTH AND SOUTH,
WHILE A DARK, BARE STONE CORRIDOR SNAKES EAST
30
THIS IS GMOG'S ROOM.  FROM THE LOOKS OF THINGS THIS GMOG FELLOW
HAD QUITE SOME INFLUENCE.  THE WALLS ARE FRESCOED WITH ANCIENT
PAINTINGS AND SCULPTURE, PRICELESS IN THEIR ANTIQUITY.  PASSAGES
LEAD NORTH, EAST AND WEST.
31
YOU ARE STANDING IN THE ROOM OF GMOG'S DOOR, WITH A SHIMMERING,
INSUBSTANTIAL VEIL OF BLANKNESS TO THE SOUTHWEST. THERE IS
NOTHING ELSE IN THE CHAMBER.  THERE ARE EXITS DOWN AND NORTHEAST.
32
YOU HAVE WALKED THROUGH A LONG CORRIDOR, ONCE PROTECTED BY A NOW-SAGGING
DOOR OF TWENTY-FOOT THICK DOOR, TO ENTER GMOG'S VAULT.  THE WALLS ARE
PITTED AND SCORED AS THE RESULT OF AN ANCIENT EXPLOSION, AND STEEL
STALACTITES DRIP FROM THE ROOF, FIFTY FEET UP, ALMOST TO THE FLOOR.
WIDE PASSAGES LEAD NORTH AND SOUTH, WITH A LOW CRAWL TO THE EAST.
33
YOU HAVE ENTERED THE GLORY OF GMOG ROOM.  A SPLENDID CHAMBER, WITH
LUSH CARPETING, HEAVY DRAPES, AN INLAID PLATINUM FLOOR
AND SIGNS PROCLAIMING "WE LOVE YOU, GMOG", IT IS DOMINATED BY A LARGE
MARBLE PLATFORM SURMOUNTED BY A MAJESTIC THRONE.  GMOG IS NOWHERE TO BE 
SEEN.  A CARVED ARCHWAY LEADS TO A LOW CORRIDOR TO THE NORTH.
34
YOU ARE IN THE LAST ROOM.  PASSAGES LEAD OUT IN *ALL* DIRECTIONS.
35
YOU ARE AT THE BEND IN AN ANGLED PASSAGE.  BRANCHES LEAD 
EAST AND NORTH.
36
YOU ARE IN A SMALL, UNLIT VESTIBULE THE SIZE OF A CLOSET.
SET DEEP IN THE NORTHERN WALL OF THE ROOM IS A THICK PLATE OF TRANSPARENT
QUARTZ FORMING A LARGE WINDOW OVERLOOKING THE SURFACE OF DEIMOS.
THE VAST CRIMSON FACE OF MARS TURNS SLOWLY PAST, BRINGING A RUDDY TINGE TO
THE COLD ROCK FACE OF THE SATELLITE SURFACE, JUST OUTSIDE.
A SIGN ON THE WALL IN MIDDLE HIGH MARTIAN READS,"ZAP!".  THERE IS A
NARROW TUNNEL IN ONE WALL LEADING SOUTH.
