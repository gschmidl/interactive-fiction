- [Cave Fun (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](ND-100%20games/Cave%20Fun%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - Mikael Johansson's 1981 adventure: a fortress behind an iron door with 27 treasures to bring home.
- [DOD (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](ND-100%20games/DOD%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - A short Swedish dungeon game (1983): lead your company of fifteen out of the caves of evil.
- [Legend (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](ND-100%20games/Legend%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - LEGEND v10.0 (1984-87), a Swedish multi-player fantasy game with the club's own worlds.
- [Mordor (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](ND-100%20games/Mordor%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - *Mordor: Land of evil* (1985): carry the Ring through Middle Earth to Mount Doom.
- [My World (ND-100, SINTRAN III)](ND-100%20games/My%20World%20%28ND-100%2C%20SINTRAN%20III%29/) - Mikael Johansson's MY_WORLD (1983): a mountain, a troll's bridge and a pirate's maze of tunnels.

## [PDP-10 games](PDP-10%20games/)

- [Lands of Zarast (PDP-10, TYMCOM-X)](PDP-10%20games/Lands%20of%20Zarast%20%28PDP-10%2C%20TYMCOM-X%29/) - A Dungeons & Dragons-style adventure in Tymshare BASIC by "Sauron the Feared" (1984-88).
- [The Monastery (PDP-10, TYMCOM-X, BASIC)](PDP-10%20games/The%20Monastery%20%28PDP-10%2C%20TYMCOM-X%2C%20BASIC%29/) - An unfinished Tymshare BASIC adventure (1987) in a ruined monastery with a ghoul and a hobbit.
